Destava

Destava is an AI career manager. It does the career work, the job search, the résumé, the applications, so you can do your best work. The app is at destava.com. No gimmicks, no daily limits, no trial clock.

What is open here

  • destava-mcp: the job search MCP server at destava.com/mcp. Three read-only tools, search_jobs, get_job and watch_search, over postings read from companies' own career sites. No account needed. Works in Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and VS Code.
  • claude-skills: agent skills, among them destava-job-search.

For people
